# User research plan

## Objective

Test whether a replay-and-journal workflow helps intended users understand historical simulation assumptions and review their own decision process.

## Research questions

1. How do learners currently practice and review hypothetical market decisions?
2. Which parts of replay, planning, and journaling are difficult or valuable?
3. Do users understand that historical results do not predict future outcomes?
4. Can users find and explain data provenance, quality labels, and modeled costs?
5. What privacy expectations apply to a personal decision journal?

## Method

- Conduct semi-structured interviews with a small, varied group of adult learners and practitioners.
- Use a clickable prototype with synthetic data; do not request account credentials or personal financial records.
- Ask participants to complete replay, hypothetical-plan, and review tasks, then explain the assumptions shown.
- Record consented notes without unnecessary identifying details.

## Recruitment and ethics

Recruitment criteria, consent language, and participant compensation are undecided. Do not target vulnerable users or imply financial outcomes. Participation must be voluntary; do not collect broker account data, credentials, or sensitive personal financial information.

## Synthesis

Separate observations, quotes (with permission), interpretations, and open questions. Record sample limitations and negative findings. Do not claim representativeness from a small convenience sample.

## Status

No interviews or usability sessions have been conducted or validated in this repository yet.
