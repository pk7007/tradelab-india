# External-source and procurement plan — 20 November 2026

## Buying decision

Use the existing paid Kite connection first. Do not buy duplicate broker data, enterprise exchange feeds, cloud AI or hosting to compensate for missing integration code. Continuous paper processing needs a scheduler and verified session/data contracts, not a second broker key. Costs below are public list information checked during this audit, not invoices or contract quotes. Taxes, entitlements and negotiated terms must be confirmed before purchase.

The ₹1,000 allocation makes recurring subscriptions material: Kite's published ₹500 monthly data fee equals 50% of the allocation per month before trading costs. Research and infrastructure expenses must be tracked separately from portfolio P&L. Brokerage-credit schemes are not a reason to increase trading activity. [Official Kite pricing](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/kite-api/articles/what-are-the-charges-for-kite-apis).

## Integration contracts and acceptance

### Market data: medium-to-high engineering effort, no new subscription proposed

Kite quotes are streamed observations, not an archive guarantee. The published WebSocket interface supports instrument subscriptions; historical bars use instrument tokens and explicit intervals/dates. Broker timestamps must be normalized with receipt timestamps separately recorded. History coverage varies by instrument; futures token rollover and expired instruments require separate records. The current app deliberately supports current NSE equities, not all exchange assets. [Kite WebSocket](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/websocket/), [historical API](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/historical/).

Respect endpoint-specific request budgets. Kite documents quote requests at 1/sec, historical at 3/sec and other endpoint/order limits separately; fetch the current limits before deployment rather than reusing a global quota. Tokens expire and require the documented login process; no refresh-token capability is assumed. [Rate limits](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/exceptions/), [authentication](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/user/).

Acceptance: exact token/symbol/product mapping; completed-bar cutoff; exchange and receipt freshness; duplicate/revised/gap replay; reconnect/expiry recovery; no orders from a read test; documented retention rights. Archive completeness, price adjustments, AI use and redistribution are not implied by the subscription. Dhan is a documented alternative with daily/intraday OHLC endpoints, but its instrument mapping, access-token lifecycle, historical ranges and usage rights need separate qualification. [Dhan historical contract](https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/historical-data/).

### Calendar: medium effort

Record exchange, segment, date, session open/close, source version and when the notice became available. A weekday heuristic must never stand in for holiday/special-session coverage. Acceptance: ordinary day, holiday, special session, changed notice, missing date, late notice and timezone boundaries all tested. Current `market_contract.session_for` is an offline validator, not a verified importer or scheduler.

### News: medium effort for RSS context; high for enterprise retention

RSS is a rolling latest feed with potential delay and no historical point-in-time promise. No verified public polling quota was established; the existing reader conservatively caches for five minutes. First-seen memory resets on restart and cannot reconstruct historical availability. Acceptance: empty/changed feed, delayed item, duplicate, malformed link, exact/unmapped/ambiguous equity name, outage/stale status and no future information in historical research. NSE source updates/retractions are not a guaranteed revision stream; do not claim such coverage.

Moneycontrol's supplied mock contract excludes real-time depth/ticks. Use delayed snapshots for context only. Before activation, provider must specify inclusive/exclusive cursor boundaries, page exhaustion, recovery after a >48-hour outage, corrected full-item retrieval, reinstatement, article schema/versioning and cancellation/retention obligations. New tests deliberately refuse to certify a full page as a completed revision sweep. Acceptance includes crash/rollback, concurrent cursor conflict, retraction arriving after update, repeated inclusive updates, stale resurrection, content removal in derivatives/backups and six-hour health failure. No closed-browser purge SLA exists without an independently running monitored service. Backups/SSD erasure remain operational concerns, not solved by SQL DELETE.

### Fundamentals/corporate actions: high effort

Require original filing date, publication/availability, reporting period, units, restatements, point-in-time ticker/sector identity, delistings and split/dividend treatment. Normalized vendor data does not automatically supply historical as-known vintages. Acceptance: sample NSE/BSE large/small/delisted names, corrections, negative bases, consolidated/standalone differences, matched dividends/contributions and total-return benchmark. Ask vendors for storage, internal AI, derived work, correction and redistribution terms; personal plan pricing is not enterprise permission.

### AI and operations: optional model, required reliability work

Do not add an LLM until deterministic research and cited evidence work. Test citation fabrication, provider failure, malicious prose, outdated sources and prohibited control fields through `evidence.py`. Local inference still needs a model licence, hardware test and content policy. Execution must remain independent. Hosting requires measured uptime, clock synchronization, secret storage, deployment rollback and private networking. Monitoring acceptance: a disconnected feed or stranded exit alerts an operator even when the HTTP server remains up; process restart must remain disarmed.

## Practical sequence

1. Keep existing Kite, formalize data-use/retention and calendar provenance, then implement the completed-candle scheduler with a bounded one-account paper pilot.
2. Record real forward observations under frozen experimental rules; compare after costs and retain failures. No new capital or subscriptions until results and data gaps justify them.
3. Qualify Moneycontrol's private protocol only after its actual local configuration and revision semantics are supplied. Long-term fundamentals and optional AI come after the core paper path is dependable.
