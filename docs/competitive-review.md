# Competitive review

## Purpose

Understand adjacent workflows and alternatives without claiming a unique market gap before collecting evidence.

## Initial review scope

- OpenAlgo and related public projects, reviewed from their current public documentation and repositories.
- Broker-provided paper-trading or chart replay features, where terms and availability can be verified.
- Spreadsheets, notebooks, and manual journaling as non-product alternatives.

## Comparison questions

- What workflows support replay, hypothetical plans, journaling, and review?
- How are data sources, timestamps, corporate actions, and limitations described?
- Which assumptions and costs can a user inspect or reproduce?
- What accessibility, privacy, and export options are documented?
- Are product claims clearly separated from historical or synthetic results?

## Evidence log

For each reviewed item, record date, official source URL, product/version, observed behavior, evidence, and limitations. Do not infer capabilities from marketing copy alone. Respect terms and do not scrape restricted content.

No comparative research has been completed. No claim of a unique gap or competitive advantage is validated.
