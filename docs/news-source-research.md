# Indian stock news: sources to research

Use [NSE corporate announcements](https://www.nseindia.com/companies-listing/corporate-filings-announcements) to check an issuer's actual disclosure. For reporting and context, compare the markets pages from [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/), [ET Markets](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets) and [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets). These are research links in the News Monitor. None is configured as an automated feed.

Reusable research prompt:

> Find sources for TradeLab India's NSE/BSE watchlist news monitor. Check the publisher's own pages: start with exchange filings, SEBI announcements, Moneycontrol, ET Markets and Business Standard. Find additional Indian business publications or licensed suppliers. For each source, give the official page, audience, coverage by symbol or exchange, timestamp and timezone, delay, corrections policy, exact documented RSS/API endpoint if any, cost or authentication, and terms for automated retrieval, storage and redistribution. Link evidence for each claim; use “unverified” rather than inferring rights from public access. Distinguish original filings from editorial reporting and syndicated copies. Rank candidates for personal local monitoring by timeliness, coverage, rights and reliability. For a permitted sample, show how to map title, HTTPS article URL, publication time, first-available time and exact symbols into TradeLab. Include test cases for duplicate stories, ambiguous tickers, later corrections, stale news and outages. Keep unapproved sources manual. Do not infer a stock-price move or place an order from a headline.
